## 1. Hypothesis (why this is not a repeat of the negative VGG result)

The earlier VGG stage was rejected on **mipnerf360/bicycle**, whose delivered
LPIPS is **0.3030 — above the 0.25 scoring cliff**, so its LPIPS term already
scores 0 and the extra stage could only cost PSNR/SSIM. The DL3DV "bad cases" sit
**just below the cliff**, where LPIPS is the dominant scoring term
(`40·clamp((0.25−LPIPS)/0.20)`):

| case | delivered LPIPS | below cliff by | LPIPS points available |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| dl3dv/0b5635d5 | 0.2461 | 0.0039 | 0.78 |
| dl3dv/0f622ea | 0.2290 | 0.0210 | 4.20 |
| dl3dv/3e4dbe41 | 0.2297 | 0.0203 | 4.06 |
| mipnerf360/counter | 0.1808 | 0.0692 | 13.84 (deep-below control) |

Bicycle measured at 200 steps: **ΔLPIPS −0.0119, ΔPSNR −0.13 dB, ΔSSIM −0.005,
stage 2.84 s**. Projected for 0b5635: LPIPS 0.2461→0.234 ⇒ **+2.4 LPIPS points**,
−0.10 PSNR ⇒ −0.08, −0.39 SSIM ⇒ −0.31, so Δq ≈ **+1.9**; ΔB ≈ +2.84 s ⇒ TIME
−0.66 ⇒ **Δtotal ≈ +1.2**. If the LPIPS gain transfers, the VGG direction is a
second positive arm; if it does not transfer, the direction is closed for good.
The counter case (deeper below the cliff) tests whether the effect scales with
LPIPS headroom.
