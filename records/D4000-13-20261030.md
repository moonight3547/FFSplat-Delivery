## 5. Caveats

* Cross-host comparison (N1 D4000 vs H1 D3500) mixes hosts/GPUs and run-to-run noise; only
  bicycle/0b5635 have same-node D3500 controls. Treat +0.070 as "indistinguishable".
* Single run per case for the 13 (as tasked) — per-case q noise ±0.3–0.5, B noise
  ±0.5–1.5 s; the 0b5635 repeat showed ±0.45 total spread, which is exactly the size of the
  D4000-vs-D3500 difference.
* Bicycle's gate fires (train-LPIPS 0.5246) and its LPIPS stays above the 0.25 cliff
  (0.2149 → 0 points for LPIPS) — its +7.45 gain comes from PSNR/SSIM + B, not the LPIPS term.
* Official-Adam compatibility cells are single-run; the −6.96 % B must be confirmed with
  repeats and on the two DL3DV cases before any recipe change.
